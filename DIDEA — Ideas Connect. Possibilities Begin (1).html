How DIDEA works

Four steps from a thought to a conversation.

  1. Share an ideaDescribe the problem, your proposed solution, and what stage you are at.
  2. Get feedbackMembers comment, upvote, and challenge assumptions respectfully.
  3. Find collaboratorsSend or receive collaboration requests with a short message.
  4. Develop itRefine your concept and explore research and innovation topics.

Community guidelines

Be original and credit others

Share your own work and attribute sources.

Give respectful feedback

Challenge ideas, not people.

Protect confidential information

Don’t post private data or anything you lack permission to share. Publishing an idea does not create patent rights or legal protection.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to browse?

No. Public ideas will be readable by everyone. An account will be needed to publish, vote, comment, and request collaboration.

Is DIDEA live?

This is the DIDEA front page. Accounts, publishing, and the database are not connected yet.

Will my idea be legally protected?

No. Posting publicly does not create legal protection. Avoid sharing confidential details.

Can I message collaborators?

Collaboration requests come first. Private messaging is not planned for the first version.

Ready to share your thinking?

Accounts open when the backend is connected.
